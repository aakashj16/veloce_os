## Follow-up incident: NVIDIA boot hang (2026-11-23–24)

On HW-01, Debian 13 kernel `6.12.118+deb13-amd64` hung at startup with `nouveau` MMIO faults and DMAR faults. GRUB and HP diagnostics remained accessible. Temporarily appending `modprobe.blacklist=nouveau` enabled an Intel-driven KDE/Wayland boot; `lspci -nnk` showed Intel `i915` in use, with NVIDIA MX130 detected but no active driver. The owner saved `GRUB_CMDLINE_LINUX_DEFAULT="quiet modprobe.blacklist=nouveau"`, ran `sudo update-grub`, and confirmed a subsequent normal reboot succeeded without GRUB edits. This is an **Intel-only workaround**, not proof of MX130 hardware failure or a complete hybrid-GPU test.

An independent HP **System Temperature (90D)** warning occurred once on 2026-11-23. The cooling system was cleaned on 2026-11-24, after which the owner reported quieter fans and a CPU reading around 58–59°C; the graphics boot problem persisted until the blacklist workaround was applied. The thermal and driver events have **no confirmed causal connection**. Full recovery, verification, rollback and remaining test obligations are documented in [the troubleshooting record](../troubleshooting.md). Graphics validation remains open.
